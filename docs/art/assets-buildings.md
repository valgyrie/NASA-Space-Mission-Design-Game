# Building model assets

Third-party assets in `public/models/buildings.glb` (textures are resized, re-encoded as JPG and embedded; colour maps of
some are turned grey so materials can tint them). Everything else in the models is made in Blender by
`scripts/blender/` (geometry, and the procedural PV-module, chain-link fence and fan-guard textures).
