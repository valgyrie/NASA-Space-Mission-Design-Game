# Campaign follows real space history, starting in the US

The Campaign starts in 1958 and moves through real Eras to Artemis IV. Each Era's rockets, technology and Milestones match what really existed. The player's Company is a fictional contractor, starting anywhere in the US by default. Early launches are rented on real rockets at real spaceports, the way private companies actually did it (AT&T paid NASA to launch Telstar 1). The Company only gets its own rockets and Spaceport in the 1990s Era, when private rockets really appeared.

**Considered options:** start anywhere on Earth as the default. Rejected because NASA hired US companies, so a foreign contractor in 1958 breaks the history the Campaign is meant to teach. Players can still choose "Anywhere on Earth", clearly labelled as alternate history. Free Play is not tied to history and allows any start.

**Consequences:** Era data (rockets, parts, contracts, dates) must be researched and cited. A change to real-world plans (e.g. Artemis IV) means updating one data file.
