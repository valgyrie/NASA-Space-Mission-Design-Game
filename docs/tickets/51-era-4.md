# 51: Era 4, telescopes and Moon maps (1990–2009)

**What to build:** the HQ Upgrade after Era 3 moves the Company into Era 4. Two Milestones: Hubble and the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter. Lunar orbit, the Space Shuttle and Atlas V, lithium batteries, and the biggest Assembly Hall. Asked for by the team on 15 Nov 2026.

**Blocked by:** 50

**Status:** planned (15 Nov 2026)

**Design (decided by the agent while the team was away; facts in `docs/research/milestones.md`, numbers are game estimates unless cited):**

- **Era 4 starts 1 January 1990.** HQ level 4. Saturn V and Delta 900 retire; the Space Shuttle and Atlas V 401 open (Atlas V first flew in 2002: it shows "Opens in 2002" until the Game date reaches it).
- **Hubble.** Client NASA. A **Space Telescope** Payload in Low Earth Orbit, carried up by the Space Shuttle (STS-31, Discovery; whole launch paid by NASA). Needs Reaction Wheels and a steady aim (no spin). Days of Data from the real mission (research decides). The "after" card tells the real flaw: the main mirror was ground to the wrong shape, found two months after launch, fixed by astronauts in December 1993. It links to the Ground Tests idea: test the whole thing before it flies.
- **Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO).** Client NASA. A **Moon Mapper** (camera and laser altimeter) in a low **lunar orbit** over the poles. Launch on Atlas V 401 from Cape Canaveral; the Spacecraft's own Main Engine brakes into lunar orbit. One year of Data (LRO's first, exploration year). Its "after" card: LCROSS, launched with it, hit a crater near the south pole and found water.
- **New Parts:** Space Telescope, Moon Mapper, Lithium Battery (Battery: much lighter per Wh), Advanced Computer, Very Large Bus (Assembly Hall level 5). Each with mass, power, cost, build time, Factory, Market and a 3D model.
- **Rockets:** Space Shuttle (orbiter, external tank, two solid boosters; LC-39B), Atlas V 401 (SLC-41). The Launch show plays their real stages; the Shuttle's orbiter carries the Spacecraft in its bay and lets it go.
- **Assembly Hall level 5** (Era 4) takes the Very Large Bus; its model grows again (a high bay with a big door).
- **Space view:** Spacecraft in lunar orbit circle the Moon.
- **Side story card:** Pegasus (1990), the first air-launched orbital rocket: private rockets begin.
- **Demo Mode:** an Era 4 save.

**Art (Blender):** Space Shuttle stack, Atlas V 401, LC-39B with its service structures, SLC-41, the new Parts, the level 5 Assembly Hall.
