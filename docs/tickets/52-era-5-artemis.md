# 52: Era 5, reusable rockets and Artemis (2015–2028)

**What to build:** the last Campaign Era, up to the finale. Milestones: the first Falcon 9 booster landing, Artemis I, Artemis II, then **Artemis III and Artemis IV, where the player chooses how the Company helps**. After the finale the Campaign continues as Free Play, which gets the same Moon Contracts. Asked for by the team on 15 Nov 2026 ("for Artemis 3 and Artemis 4, give the player a choice to do whatever he wants, in the campaign and in Free Play").

**Blocked by:** 51

**Status:** planned (15 Nov 2026)

**Design (decided by the agent while the team was away; facts in `docs/research/milestones.md`, re-check Artemis dates before release, they are in the future):**

- **Era 5 starts 1 January 2015** in the Campaign (Free Play keeps starting on 1 January 2026). HQ level 5. The Space Shuttle retires (last flew 2011); Falcon 9, Falcon Heavy (from 2018) and SLS (from 2022) open.
- **Falcon 9 booster landing.** A communications satellite on Falcon 9, like the eleven ORBCOMM satellites of December 2015. After it: the booster landed, so **reused boosters** make Falcon 9 Rideshares cheaper from then on (game estimate).
- **Artemis I** (support). A **CubeSat** riding on SLS with the uncrewed Orion, as ten real CubeSats did, out past the Moon. New Bus: **CubeSat (6U)**. A radiation detector out there links back to Explorer 1.
- **Artemis II** (support). The crewed flight around the Moon (April 2026). The Company's part is chosen from what really flew or helped (research decides; e.g. a CubeSat or tracking).
- **Artemis III: your choice.** In 2027 a crew tests docking with a Moon lander in Earth orbit (the plan since February 2026). The board offers three ways to help, all game ideas built on real needs; the player picks one (or more for extra reward): a **camera satellite** that watches the docking, a **relay** in Earth orbit, or a **radiation watch** for the crew. Any one completes the Milestone.
- **Artemis IV: your choice (finale).** The planned first crewed landing since 1972, near the Moon's south pole, by 2028. Three ways to help: a **lunar relay satellite** in orbit (the south pole often cannot see Earth), a **cargo lander** that sets a science package down near the landing site, or a **Moon Mapper** that scouts the landing site. Any one finishes the Campaign; a closing card, then "Continue in Free Play" with the same Company. The other choices stay as optional Contracts.
- **New Parts:** CubeSat Bus (6U), Lander (legs and a landing engine, for the surface), Relay Radio (Comms: talks to other Spacecraft). 3D models for each.
- **Rockets:** SLS Block 1 with Orion (LC-39B mobile launcher), Falcon Heavy (three cores; side boosters land). Falcon 9's first stage lands in the show after the booster-landing Milestone.
- **Free Play:** generated Moon Contracts (lunar relay, lunar mapping, cargo to the surface) and the three Artemis choices as Contract kinds, once the Company has the Parts. The Weekly Challenge is unchanged.
- **Demo Mode:** an Era 5 save.

**Art (Blender):** SLS with Orion and its mobile launcher, Falcon Heavy, the Falcon 9 booster landing, CubeSat, lander, relay satellite.
