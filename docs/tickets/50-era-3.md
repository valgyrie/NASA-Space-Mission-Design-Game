# 50: Era 3, the Moon and the land from above (1969–1972)

**What to build:** the HQ Upgrade after Syncom 3 moves the Company into Era 3. Two Milestones arrive: the science package the Apollo 11 crew set up on the Moon, and Landsat 1. New orbits (the Moon's surface, Sun-synchronous), new Parts, Saturn V and Delta 900, Kennedy's LC-39 and Vandenberg, and a bigger Assembly Hall. Real History cards before and after each. Asked for by the team on 15 Nov 2026 ("continue the Eras until Artemis IV").

**Blocked by:** 18

**Status:** planned (15 Nov 2026)

**Design (decided by the agent while the team was away; facts in `docs/research/milestones.md`, numbers are game estimates unless cited):**

- **Era 3 starts 1 January 1969.** HQ level 3. Thor-Delta and Delta D retire ("Retired: last flew in …"); Delta 900 and Saturn V open.
- **Apollo 11 science package** (support Milestone). Client NASA. The player builds the package the crew set up at Tranquility Base: a **Science Package** Payload like EASEP (a seismometer with its own solar panels and a laser retroreflector). It rides in the Lunar Module on a Saturn V that NASA pays for, so the Launch costs the Company nothing; Shipping goes to Kennedy. Destination: the **Moon's surface**. Data: **21 days**, as long as the real seismometer worked. On the Moon a day lasts about two Earth weeks, then two weeks of night: solar power alone stops at nightfall, so the player trades battery against panels (the real one ran on solar power). On the surface the Spacecraft does not need to spin: Pointing is not asked.
- **Landsat 1.** Client NASA. A **Multispectral Scanner** (four colour bands) in a **Sun-synchronous orbit** (about 900 km, tilted about 99°, so it passes over each place at the same local time). Launch on **Delta 900** from **Vandenberg** (rockets to polar orbits fly south over the Pacific, not east over the Atlantic). It points at Earth all the time: needs **Reaction Wheels** (three-axis pointing). Days of Data: from the real design life or first years of work (research decides; cite it).
- **New Parts:** Science Package, Multispectral Scanner, Reaction Wheels (Pointing), Dish Antenna (Comms: fast, but must point at Earth), Fold-out Solar Panels (Power: more watts than body-mounted cells), Heavy Bus (Assembly Hall level 4). Each has mass, power, cost, build time, Factory and Market price like the others, and a 3D model.
- **Rockets and Launch Sites:** Saturn V (three stages; Kennedy LC-39A; whole-launch only, paid by NASA for the Apollo Contract), Delta 900 (Thor with nine strap-on solid boosters; Vandenberg and the Cape). Launch Site **Kennedy (LC-39)** and **Vandenberg**. The Launch show plays each rocket's real stages; for the Moon it adds "Off to the Moon" after the last burn and ends on the Moon.
- **Assembly Hall level 4** (Era 3) takes the Heavy Bus; its model grows (taller high bay).
- **Space view and Test Run:** a package on the Moon's surface shows on the Moon; Sun-synchronous orbits show tilted past the pole.
- **Demo Mode:** an Era 3 save (Apollo 11 package accepted, a design ready).
- **Optional if time allows:** Propellant Plant (GDD §5: water → propellant, Era 3).

**Art (Blender, by script, credited like the other models):** Saturn V, Delta 900, the LC-39 pad (mobile launcher with its tower, flame trench, crawlerway), Vandenberg SLC-2W, the new Parts, the bigger Assembly Hall.
